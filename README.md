Privacy Policy
Friendfinder — Last updated: 5 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Friendfinder ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using Friendfinder you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect
Information you provide
Account details — email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth)
Profile information — name, date of birth, gender, religion, ethnicity, bio
Profile photo — if you choose to upload one
Interests — the interests you select in the app
Messages — the content of messages you send to other users
Information collected automatically
Precise location — collected only while the app is in the foreground, used to show nearby users
Device push token — used to send you notifications (e.g. new messages, friend requests)
2. How We Use Your Information
Show your profile to nearby users so they can connect with you
Display other users' profiles to you based on your location and filters
Enable real-time messaging between connected users
Send push notifications for messages and friend requests
Improve app performance and debug issues
3. Location Data
We collect your device's GPS coordinates to find other Friendfinder users near you. Your exact location is never shown to other users. Other users only see an approximate distance (e.g. "2 km away"). Location is only accessed while the app is open (foreground use). We do not track your location in the background.

4. Who We Share Your Information With
Other users — your profile (name, photo, bio, interests, approximate distance) is visible to other Friendfinder users near you. Messages you send are visible to the recipient only.
Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy
Stream — our real-time chat provider. Messages are processed via Stream's infrastructure. Stream Privacy Policy
Google Firebase — used for push notification delivery. Firebase Privacy Policy
Expo — used for push notification token management. Expo Privacy Policy
We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Retention
We retain your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your profile, photos, messages, and location history will be deleted within 30 days. To request account deletion, contact us at the email below.

6. Security
All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure users can only access data they are authorised to see.

7. Children's Privacy
Friendfinder is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

8. Your Rights
You have the right to:

Access the personal data we hold about you
Correct inaccurate data
Request deletion of your data
Withdraw consent for location access at any time via your device settings
9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via a notice in the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
